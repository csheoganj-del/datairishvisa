## SRC-003 — ISD Join Family in Ireland Policy

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Title** | Coming to Join Family in Ireland |
| **Institution** | Immigration Service Delivery (ISD), Department of Justice |
| **URL** | https://www.irishimmigration.ie/coming-to-join-family-in-ireland/ |
| **Last accessed** | 4 October 2026 |
| **Key revision** | 12 June 2026 (financial threshold change) |

### Key Verified Facts

- **Category A sponsors** (Irish citizens): Financial requirement — gross cumulative income of **€75,000 over the previous 3 years**. Effective from 12 June 2026 (previously €40,000 cumulative).
- Single income only — cannot combine with partner's income.
- Sponsor must not have relied mainly on State supports for 2+ continuous years prior to application.
- **Category B sponsors** (CSEP, Hosting Agreement, etc.): No waiting period before family can apply.
- **Category C sponsors** (General Employment Permit, etc.): 12 months employment in Ireland required before family can apply.

|-------------|----------------|
| Critical Skills Employment Permit (CSEP) | Received up to **25 September 2026** (~1 week) |
| New General Employment Permit | Received up to **26 August 2026** (~5-6 weeks) |

### Nurses and Employment Permits

- Registered nurses (RGN, etc.) are listed on the **Critical Skills Occupations List**.
- A fully NMBI-registered nurse can be employed on a **Critical Skills Employment Permit**.
- Nurses completing an **Adaptation Period** (not yet fully registered) may work under the **Atypical Working Scheme (AWS)** administered by the Department of Justice.
- General Employment Permit also available for nursing roles in some circumstances.

## SRC-006 — NMBI Registration Process

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Title** | Registration Process for Overseas Nurses |
| **Institution** | Nursing and Midwifery Board of Ireland (NMBI) |
| **URL** | https://www.nmbi.ie/Registration/Registration-Process |
| **Last accessed** | 4 October 2026 |

### Key Process Steps

1. **Application via MyNMBI portal** — must be personal; agents prohibited
2. **Qualification Recognition Assessment** — degree and transcripts assessed against Irish standards
3. **English Language Proficiency** — IELTS Academic or OET; single-sitting requirement
4. **Compensation Measures** (if gap identified):
   - Period of Adaptation and Assessment (6-12 weeks supervised practice) — candidate secures own placement
   - Aptitude Test (General and Psychiatric nursing only, via RCSI)
5. **12 months** from decision letter to complete compensation measure

## SRC-007 — EU Directive 2004/38/EC (Irish Transposition)

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Title** | European Communities (Free Movement of Persons) Regulations 2015 |
| **Reference** | S.I. No. 548 of 2015 (as amended by S.I. No. 445 of 2021) |
| **Institution** | Government of Ireland / Department of Justice |
| **URL** | https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/eli/2015/si/548/made/en/print |
| **EU Source** | https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=celex%3A32004L0038 |
| **Last accessed** | 4 October 2026 |

### Key Legal Points

- EEA/Swiss nationals exercising free movement in Ireland may bring qualifying family members.
- **Irish citizens living in Ireland CANNOT use the EU Directive** — they are not "exercising free movement."
- Qualifying family members: spouse/civil partner, direct descendants under 21 or dependent, dependent ascendants.
- Permitted family members (case-by-case): durable partners, dependent extended family.
- Processing: via Residence Card application (Irish Naturalisation and Immigration Service).

### Key Facts (October 2026)

- VFS Global is the official visa application centre partner in India.
- Applications submitted to VFS Delhi typically reach Embassy the next working day.
- Study visa processing: approximately **45 calendar days** for complete applications.
- Recommended lead time: **8–12 weeks** before travel.
- No individual application status updates provided by Embassy.
- **Appeals abolished (1 June 2026):** Short-stay (Type C) visa refusal appeals no longer accepted. New application required.
